Overview

The Course Savers iOS app helps keep college costs transparent so that students can better manage their school loans and college debt. With help in class selection, budgeting and an app monitored, secured credit card, the Course Savers app helps students spend wiser.

The Problem

Student debt is stifling American graduates.
And a considerable portion of student debt comes from students overborrowing and spending loan money on non-essentials such as expensive cars, clothes & brand new phones.

Research Methods

Interviews

I interviewed 4 N. American college students ages 18 - 25, all either in school or 1 year graduated.

Secondary

I compiled supporting data from at least 5 different sources.

User Tests

I tested the mobile solution on 10 different users who fit the same interview criteria in person or on zoom.

Priorities

What to Fix First?

Looking at test results, I used this matrix to rank the biggest but easiest problems.

  • P0 - Users don’t recognize function of cart icon
  • P1 - Users unfamiliar with financial terms
  • P2 - Users confused by strikethrough text
  • P3 - Academic Map Icon is misleading
  • P4 - Users skipped class selection

P0 - Though the cart icon may be the hardest to fix, it is given high priority because it could prevent users from completing the task of registering for courses altogether.

Hidden Button

Between mobile V1 & V3, the register button was made to be hidden until a course is actually selected so that users don’t skip steps.

Ambiguous Cart

V1 originally had a shopping cart icon to add courses. But the 1st user overlooking it quickly proved how that may have been too ambiguous. The clear message, “Add,” was used instead.

Impact

Course Savers could...

​

  • help schools sell a more convenient & modern registration experience.
  • lower spending on advisor and financial bursar staff.
  • lower dropout rates by offering mobile guidance.
  • lessen the amount of student non-essential spending.
  • save students from predatory credit card lenders.
  • help students build credit safely.

Key Learnings

  • Colleges often have semi hidden costs. (NYT)
  • Be careful with ambiguous icons.
  • Progressive disclosure aids comprehension.
  • A user story is kind of like a product feature.
    & an epic encases several of those features.
  • Components may detach when
    copied across Figma pages.
  • Make all text styles before typography scale.
  • Redlining is tedious. Plan accordingly.
  • 1920 x 1080 doesn’t fit perfectly into the
    Figma Macbook device presets.
